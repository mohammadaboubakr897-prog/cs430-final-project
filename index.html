Welcome to Boxing Training Hub

This website teaches basic boxing ideas such as footwork, punching drills, defense, and training habits. The goal is to organize useful boxing information in a clean and simple website.

Training Focus Areas

Footwork

Footwork helps a boxer move safely, keep balance, and create better angles. Good movement is important before throwing punches.

Heavy Bag Practice

Heavy bag rounds help build power, rhythm, endurance, and punching accuracy. A boxer can practice combinations, defense, and movement on the bag.

Defense

Defense includes slipping, blocking, rolling, and moving away from punches. A strong defense helps a boxer stay calm and controlled.
